# Dungeo lore candidates

**Status:** PARKED. Not catalog canon. Not the engine.

Three earlier settings were written as if they shared a Prisma backend:

- Sugarbound: raiders stealing sacred sugar from dwarven tunnels.
- Dungeo: monsters infiltrating kitchens to take the sugar back.
- Culinary Dungeon Master: faction kitchens, pudding samurai, muffin ninjas, goulash syndicates.

That backend was not built. NCE Classic does not use sugar as a resource, stealth, crafting, or factions.

## How to use this file

If one of these images answers the creature interview — recognizable, funny, and still an elemental card — copy that judgment into `nce-classic-v01-decision-interview.md`.

Do not add these names to `classic.json` during the freeze. Do not treat sugar geology, hunger, or culinary factions as rules of the prototype.
