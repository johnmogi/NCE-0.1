# NCE concept

**Status:** narrative concept. The myth below is source material. The first playable slice is NCE Classic, described in `nce-classic-v01-checkpoint-1.md`.  
**Creatures in this file are not approved records.**

---

## 1. Three things

The Nexus Central Engine is a field guide, a game engine, and a portal between invented worlds.

Alone, it is a card database and an admin skeleton. With its maker, it is the machine Adam Douglas uses to forge a small reality and then walk into it.

## 2. The myth, kept short

John Mogi's unfinished novel *Black Gold: Reborn Shadows* had an adult Adam Douglas. Lilith was ordered to detain him and killed him instead. At the nexus point, where Shechinah rekindles a sun from the last grain of dust, the hero, the author, and Lilith all arrive. That death is the plot hole the machine is built around.

The Adam who uses the machine is twelve, in Austin, at a messy desk, on a green CRT, asking how to make an impossible game.

He starts from cards. He takes the elements seriously: air, fire, water, earth. He removes the tens in the myth because he thinks zero arrived late. He treats the jokers as the Fool and the Magician. His field guide collides with Lily's Sun and Moon notes.

That myth explains the mood. It does not set the prototype's numbers.

## 3. What LV00 became

LV00 was supposed to be the smallest construct that could still be played.

That construct now exists. It is not the sugar maze. It is:

- four Aces
- a generated 3×5 board
- a support hand
- Treasure, Barrier, Challenger, and Royal
- a win, a loss, and a restart

Dungeo remains the name of a future test world, not the name of the current rules.

## 4. Creature material, still open

The notes proposed insects, rodents, lizards, and dwarfs, crossed with an element and a second condition. A plain ant is dull. A pickled mantis with an ooze leak is the kind of joke the catalog should be able to hold.

They also proposed hero flavors: a short snobbish paladin, a cookie-stealing elf, an empty-kind mage. The shipped heroes are the four Aces only. Those flavors are not classes.

Whether creatures should be authored one by one, or generated from Family, Transformation, Culinary Treatment, and Quirk, is the open dataset question. It is asked in `nce-classic-v01-decision-interview.md`. This concept file does not answer it.

## 5. Later on purpose

Hybrid heroes with a second element. Extra tarot decks. The Dusk deck. The L.A.B. story of Richard Douglas and the vortex. A menu that claims it can end existence. Rooms that mutate after you leave. Suits as a visible skin.

## 6. Principles that still apply

- A small rule should be explainable in the fiction, but the fiction does not silently become the rule.
- A contradiction between documents is a planning problem, not a portal that authorizes two mechanics at once.
- Humor and danger can share a card. The card still needs an element and a target the player can read.
- The first build has to be playable before the dataset grows. That build is frozen until it has been played.

## 7. Open myth questions

These can stay open without blocking playtest:

- Which Adam is using the machine?
- Is Shechinah a goddess, a system, or both?
- What does the Viper know?
- Are the Fool and the Magician the champions, or something else?
- Is Lily a second author, a witness, or a deck?
